Where we'd all stay 那覇 / Naha

All three sit within a short walk of Kokusai-dori and Makishi Market, so the walkable core is right outside the door. One base for the whole trip.

Hyatt Regency Naha

★ 4.4 · A block off Kokusai-dori

Larger rooms than you'd expect in Japan, international breakfast, helpful English-speaking staff, and a stone's throw from Kokusai-dori and Makishi Market. The reliable "everyone's comfortable" choice.

~$130–200 / night very rough
Best balance of space + location for a multi-generation group.

Hotel Palm Royal Resort Pool

★ 4.3 · Quiet end of Kokusai-dori

Recently refreshed rooms, a small pool with a restaurant, and a side-street spot that keeps the street noise down while staying steps from the action. Convenience stores all around.

~$90–150 / night very rough
A pool for the 8-year-old + walkable; good mid-range value.

Loisir Hotel Naha

★ 4.0 · Nishi, near the port

Bigger, resort-style hotel with an on-site hot-spring bath and spacious rooms, closest to the airport. A little further from Kokusai-dori (a short taxi or ~15-min walk), so the trade is space-and-amenities vs. being right on the street.

~$110–170 / night very rough
Pick if we want a bath/onsen and more room to spread out.

02

Good stuff for Ahma & Yehyeh 散歩 / a stroll

All on foot from the hotel, all flat, all well-signed. Heavy Taiwanese tourism here means Chinese menus and some Mandarin-speaking staff are common — and Japan's calm, orderly streets make wandering on their own genuinely low-stress.

Kokusai-dori

★ 4.4 · The main street

The lively mile-long spine of Naha: souvenir shops, snack stands, cafés, department stores, ice cream, the occasional street performer. Flat and easy to walk, lively day and night.

The anchor — they can wander it for hours and never lose their way.

Makishi Public Market

★ 4.1 · 2 min off Kokusai-dori · 8am–10pm

Pick fresh seafood downstairs and have the cooks upstairs prepare it for lunch. Pottery and glass-bead stalls too. A perfect leisurely midday anchor.

Sit-down seafood lunch with zero planning, right in the walking zone.

Heiwa-dori Arcade Shaded

★ 4.3 · Beside Kokusai-dori

A covered shopping arcade threading off the main street — souvenirs, snacks, slow browsing. The roof matters in July: shade from sun and shelter from sudden rain.

Weatherproof browsing — the hot-afternoon / rain-shower backup.

Shuri Castle Optional outing

★ 4.2 · ~20 min by monorail + walk

The Ryukyu Kingdom's hilltop royal castle, with greenery, stone-paved paths and city views. The main hall is mid-reconstruction after the 2019 fire, but the grounds are open and rewarding.

A gentle half-day with history + views if they want one trip out.
03

Good stuff for everybody 家族 / all of us

Things that work for the whole crew — including an 8-year-old. Easy water where the snorkeling is gentle, the headline aquarium, and a couple of good meals. "Easy" beaches below have netted swim zones and/or lifeguards.

Okinawa Churaumi Aquarium Must-do

★ 4.6 · Motobu, ~2 hr north · 8:30am–6:30pm

One of the world's great aquariums: the giant Kuroshio tank with whale sharks and manta rays, plus free outdoor dolphin shows, touch pools, turtles and manatees. Indoors, so it's also the perfect rainy-day plan.

The single best all-ages day. An 8-year-old will lose his mind (good way).
Official site ↗

Naminoue Beach Easiest

★ 4.0 · In Naha — short bus/taxi

The city beach: lifeguards on duty, a netted swim area (important in box-jellyfish season), calm clean water, showers and food trucks. Not wild nature — there's a highway overpass — but unbeatably easy with a kid.

Zero-effort swim within reach of the hotel. The default beach.

Zanpa / Cape Maeda area Snorkel

★ 4.6 · Onna coast, ~1 hr

Crystal water and lots of fish right off the rocks; vendors rent life jackets and floats. Gets deep fairly quickly, so the 8-year-old stays in a vest and we go at high tide — but the fish life is a big step up from the city beach.

Where the family sees real reef fish; also Chad's shore-dive backup (see §4).

Sesoko Beach Netted zone

★ 4.3 · Motobu, near aquarium · 9am–5pm

Soft sand, clear shallow water, a netted safe-swim area with small fish, plus chair/umbrella rentals. Pair it with the aquarium for one big northern day.

Combine with Churaumi → one outstanding all-ages day up north.

Kuninda Nice dinner

★ 4.8 · Off Kokusai-dori

Refined, modern Ryukyu tasting-menu cuisine with warm service that walks you through each dish. Formal-looking but reportedly welcoming to kids — book ahead and you may get a private room.

The one special family dinner. Reserve in advance.

Kokusai-dori izakayas

Casual · all over the main street

For relaxed nights: Okinawan classics like rafute (braised pork belly), goya champuru, taco rice and Orion beer at any number of casual spots right on the street. Easy, kid-friendly, no reservations.

The low-key default dinners between the bigger outings.
July water note: box jellyfish are around in summer — stick to netted swim zones with the little one, and life vests for everyone snorkeling.
04

Dive stuff for Chad 素潜り / skin diving

The whole range exists here: dedicated skin-diving (freediving) day trips, full AIDA freediving courses, and scuba-first shops that'll boat you to the Keramas. Plan is mostly freediving with a scuba day or two. Key phrase when booking: ask for "skin diving" (スキンダイビング) with real breath-hold safety — not surface snorkeling.

Kerama Islands The prize

Boat from Naha · "Kerama Blue"

World-class visibility (30m+), coral gardens, turtles. The main event — operators run their own boats out from Naha, so it's a day trip from our base.

Where Chad spends his best water days.

DIVEIN Okinawa Top pick · scuba

★ 5.0 · Tsuboya · ~10 min from hotel

Excellent English, hotel pickup in Naha, Kerama boat dives, great with first-timers and solo divers. Walking distance from the Kokusai-dori base. Fills up — email well ahead.

The easy scuba day(s). Books out early.
Site ↗

BlueVistaDive Multilingual

★ 4.9 · Urasoe · just north

Lead instructor has 10+ years on the Keramas and works in several languages including Mandarin/Chinese; hotel pickup included. Strong if a Chinese-speaking guide is useful.

Backup scuba option with language flexibility.
Site ↗

Freediving / AIDA school Breath-hold

Naha-area · apnea-specific

Dedicated freediving schools run AIDA courses with depth sessions (~20m), dynamic apnea, and proper one-up-one-down buddy safety — the right call for at least one day so the safety culture matches real breath-hold diving.

For pushing depth properly, not being capped at snorkel level.

Cape Maeda / Blue Cave Backup

★ 4.4 · Onna · ~1 hr · shore entry

Iconic shore-entry site with the Blue Cave and turtles, reachable by car/bus with no ferry. The weather plan-B when seas are too rough for the Kerama boats.

No-ferry fallback on rough days — and the family can come too.
Two caveats: (1) These are reef-diving operators, not spearfishing — SoCal-style spearing rules don't apply here, local fishing rights are restrictive, so treat this as reef freediving. (2) Keep Kerama dive days flexible in July; don't book the boat as the very last thing before flights.
